# Store listing — English

**App name (max 30):** Lawh – Quran Memorization

**Short description (max 80):** Write verses from memory and check every word. Fully offline.

**Full description:**
Lawh helps you consolidate your Quran memorization in the most honest way: writing from memory.

• Type the verse on a normal Arabic keyboard, no diacritics needed; spelling variants are normalized for you.
• Word-by-word matching: correct words in green, mistakes in red, then the verse is shown in full Uthmani script (Hafs 'an 'Asim).
• Three modes: verse by verse, free recitation of a whole surah with per-verse results, and custom ranges.
• Hint levels: none, first word, or previous verse.
• Mistakes log, statistics and daily streak.
• The complete Quran, 114 surahs, fully offline.
• Calm ornamental design with light and dark themes and adjustable text size.
• No ads, no data collection.
